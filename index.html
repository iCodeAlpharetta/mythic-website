About Me!

The newly discovered dragon

I am a dragon that was recently discovered by human kind. If you don't know, my name is Toothless and I am from England. I have existed since the middle ages and have lived a very peaceful life with humans.

How I Look!

If you were to describe me, I would come out as scaly, large, winged, etc. My kind usually have scales and are large after our childhood ages. Most dragons have wings unless you live closer to central Asia where the dragons don't have wings. Some of us though, can breath fire!
